Compliance Readiness Summary

This evidence pack documents baseline APK assessment artifacts and their traceability to OWASP MASVS, MASWE, and MASTG references. It supports integrity verification, manifest and permission review, component visibility, and dependency awareness. It does not by itself prove the absence of vulnerabilities; dynamic evidence, screenshots, and analyst findings should be added for deeper review.
